Most AI enablement programs teach people to use a chat window. Seats get activated. Prompt libraries grow. Pilots produce demos. Then someone asks why the "SEO agent" never finishes anything, and nobody can answer, because nothing in the design said when work was done.

This course teaches a different **unit of progress**: a bounded **workflow** with a named outcome, independent evidence, and an exit condition.

## The three phases

| Phase | You leave knowing |
| --- | --- |
| **1, Reframe** | Why chatbot thinking stalls; three senses of "agent"; workflow vs role |
| **2, Design** | How to spot a high-value workflow and draft a contract for it |
| **3, Operate** | How to run workflows with evidence, cost discipline, and realistic adoption |

Each item in the sidebar is one lesson. Use **Next** at the bottom to move through Phase 1 in order, or jump via the spine like an Anthropic Academy module.
