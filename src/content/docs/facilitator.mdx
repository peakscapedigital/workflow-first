## Audience

- AI enablement leads and program managers
- Team leads scoping their first workflow (not their first chatbot)
- Practitioners who need a shared vocabulary before build conversations

## When this course is the right instrument

This course addresses a **knowledge / reframe gap**: the team treats AI as a chatbot or persona, not as workflow design.

Signs the course fits:

- "Just paste it into ChatGPT" is the operating model
- Pilots produce demos, not bounded outcomes
- Nobody can name the exit condition for an "agent"

## When to escalate (different instruments)

Do not send these stalls to more training:

| Stall sounds like | Gap type | Instrument (not built here) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "I won't automate my own job" | Motivation | Safe practice, leadership narrative, incentives |
| Sanctioned path slower than shadow AI | Environment | Fix tooling, permissions, KPIs |
| Learned in pilot, reverted after 90 days | Habit | Reinforcement, changed defaults, manager checkpoints |
| Leadership says AI, manager says no | Communication | Aligned sponsor story |

Name the gap. Pick the instrument. Training is one instrument — useful here because the main stall is the wrong mental model.

## Materials checklist

- [ ] Projector or shared screen for case study diagram
- [ ] Printed or digital worksheets from [Downloads](/downloads/)
- [ ] One real "agent" idea from the team to use in Activity 1

## After the session

Each participant should leave with:

1. One classified idea (sense + workflow-or-role)
2. One drafted workflow contract (even rough)
3. One committed workflow for next quarter with a before metric

Follow up at 30 days on the committed workflow — not on "AI usage."
