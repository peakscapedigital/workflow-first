---
title: Override rate as health
description: Near-zero override is not success — it may mean fear or inability to detect errors.
---

Teams often celebrate "nobody contradicts the model." That can mean healthy trust — or **automation bias**, **psychological safety gap**, or **genuine inability to spot errors**.

A cited healthy band for consequential outputs is often **10–30% override**: enough skepticism, not constant rubber-stamping.

## Two different causes

| Observation | Possible cause | Intervention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zero override, people silent | Fear of looking wrong | Safe practice, leadership modeling correction |
| Zero override, people confident | Cannot detect errors | Skill practice, planted-error exercises |
| Override always | Grader too loose or wrong task | Fix workflow, not training |

Training on "AI limitations" addresses **knowledge**. It does not fix **motivation** or **skill** gaps — diagnose which stall you have.

## Planted-error exercises

Run a output with a deliberate mistake. Can reviewers catch it under time pressure? That measures **skill under realistic conditions**, not attendance.
