title: The productivity J-curve
description: Expect a dip before compound gains — and protect safe practice as an environment intervention.
---

AI transformation narratives show the upward slope. Operators live the dip first: new steps, new checks, new failure modes before toil drops.

That J-curve is **expected**, not a sign the initiative failed. Enablement that promises instant speed loses credibility when week three feels slower than week zero.

## Safe practice is not a training module

"Mistakes don't affect performance reviews" is an **environment** intervention — it belongs in how the org rewards experimentation, not in slide 14 of a deck.

When plans compress from ninety days to six weeks, training modules survive and environment changes get cut. Name them correctly so they survive scope negotiation.

## What to tell leadership

- Leading indicator early: **workflow completion with evidence**, not seat count
- Lagging indicator: hours saved, error rate, cycle time — on **one named workflow**
- Narrative wins come **after** one unit proves, not before
